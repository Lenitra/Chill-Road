Weave through endless traffic at 200+ km/h. Brush past cars, trucks and tractors as close as you dare: every near miss adds points and pushes your multiplier up to x10. Slow down and the combo fades. Crash, or get busted by the police, and the whole run is gone.

The road never ends. It is generated as you drive: highways, country roads, dirt tracks, roundabouts, overpasses and roadworks, under a full day/night cycle and a sky that can turn to storm at any moment.

2-player split screen

Race a friend on the same endless road, top and bottom of the screen: each player has their own car, camera, HUD and score. Two gamepads, or keyboard for player 1 and a gamepad for player 2. Fall too far behind and you are brought back into the race right behind the leader.

What's on the road

  • Near-miss scoring: the closer and faster, the bigger the payout. Driving in the oncoming lane doubles everything. Clean drifts score too.
  • Random events: speed cameras, average-speed zones, checkpoint runs, near-miss chains, drift zones, cone slaloms, no-brake stretches, oncoming challenges and duels against a rival.
  • Police chases: flash a speed camera way too fast and a cruiser comes after you. Outrun it or lose everything.
  • Storms: rain, lightning and thunder, a road that slowly gets wet, with reflections, puddles and less grip.
  • Busy traffic: about 25 vehicle types, from city cars to semis, cement mixers, fire engines, motorbikes and farm tractors.
  • Physics with some weight: suspension, tire model, gearboxes from 3 to 9 speeds, drifts that come from real sliding.
  • Live-synthesized sound: every engine is generated in real time, and so is the synthwave soundtrack. You can also drop your own .ogg/.mp3/.wav files into StreamingAssets/Music.
  • Demo mode: watch an AI driver play a real run, filmed like a trailer.
  • Your road, your rules: tune highway and dirt share, curves, hills, junctions, traffic, events and chance of rain. Keep a seed to replay a road.

9 cars, each with its own engine sound

  • A retro-futuristic hydrogen wedge concept
  • A 90s Japanese turbo coupe
  • A little roadster with pop-up headlights
  • A charcoal '69 fastback, fit for a retired hitman
  • A white-and-black 80s coupe that knows every mountain pass (and every tofu shop)
  • A stainless-steel gull-wing whose backfires come out of its time vents
  • A Swedish-style megacar with a swan-neck wing
  • A teal V12 Le Mans hypercar
  • The world's smallest production car: three wheels, one headlight, 61 km/h flat out. Good luck.

Car relays every 2 km swap you into the next one without stopping.

Made 100% with AI

Chill Road is an experiment: a complete game built by Claude (Anthropic) through Claude Code, with a human giving directions, reference photos, feedback and playtesting.

  • Code: all of it, from physics and traffic AI to weather and UI.
  • 3D models: every car, tree and building was built by AI-written scripts driving Blender.
  • Sound: engines and music are synthesized in code. There is not a single audio file in the game.
  • This page: the text, and the screenshots, taken by an AI-written camera script.

No asset packs, no third-party assets.
